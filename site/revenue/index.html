The problem

They didn't say no.
Nobody called back.

Your rep did the inspection, built the proposal and left the folder on the kitchen table. Then the week got busy. The homeowner still needs the work, and the next contractor who calls gets the conversation.

Leak 01

Leads that wait

A form comes in at 6:47 PM. Nobody sees it until morning. You paid for that lead, and the company that called back first got the appointment.

Leak 02

Estimates that go quiet

One follow-up text, maybe two, then nothing. Most lost estimates aren't lost. They're forgotten.

Leak 03

An owner who finds out late

A rep's close rate slides for a month before anyone notices. By the sales meeting, those estimates are cold.

Try this today

Pull every estimate from the last 90 days that isn't marked sold or lost. Count how many have fewer than two follow-ups logged. If the list is short, your team is doing it right. If it's long, that's what this page is about.
