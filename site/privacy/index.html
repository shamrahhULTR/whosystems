Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Who we are

The website at whosystem.com and its subdomains, including get.whosystem.com (the "Site"), is operated by MAMIM Holdings LLC, doing business as WhoSystems ("WhoSystems," "we," "us," or "our"), 900 Commonwealth Pl, Suite 200, Virginia Beach, VA 23464, phone (757) 705-5468, email hello@whosystem.com. WhoSystems installs AI lead response and follow-up systems for home-service businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect through the Site and our communications, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use our forms, booking links, calculators, or job application, we collect what you submit, such as your name, email address, mobile phone number, company and trade, messages you type, calculator inputs, your text message consent choices, and, for job applicants, your resume and application answers.

Information collected automatically. We and our service providers may automatically collect technical data such as IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, referring URLs, and general usage data through cookies, local storage (for example, your light or dark theme preference), and similar technologies, including analytics or advertising tools if enabled.

Information from scheduling and messaging tools. If you book a call through our scheduling provider or message us, we receive the information you provide through those tools.

3. How we use information

We use information to respond to your inquiry and provide requested services; schedule, confirm, and remind you about appointments; contact you by phone, email, and, only if you have opted in, text message (see Section 5); operate, secure, and improve the Site; operate automated and AI-assisted communications; evaluate job applications; measure our marketing; and comply with law.

4. Automated and AI-assisted communications

Some of our replies and follow-ups, including text messages, emails, and chat conversations, may be generated or assisted by artificial-intelligence systems operated by us or our technology providers. Messages you send us may be processed by those providers and stored in our customer-relationship-management (CRM) system with your contact details. AI-assisted responses may be inaccurate and are not professional advice. Please do not send sensitive personal information (such as government identifiers, financial account numbers, or health information) by text or website messaging.

5. Text messaging (SMS) program

We send text messages only to people who opt in. You can opt in by checking one or both of the optional, unchecked text message consent boxes on our website forms, or by giving verbal consent on a phone call with us. There are two separate choices:

  • Non-marketing texts: replies to your inquiry, appointment confirmations and reminders, and customer support.
  • Marketing texts: special offers, discounts, and service updates about WhoSystems services.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase, and you can submit our forms without checking either box. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at (757) 705-5468 or hello@whosystem.com. Reply STOP at any time to opt out; we will send one confirmation and no further texts. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. See our Terms & Conditions for the full text message program terms.

Mobile information and text message consent. No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

6. How we disclose information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not buy or sell leads. We disclose personal information only as follows:

  • Service providers that operate the Site and our communications on our behalf and under confidentiality obligations, such as website hosting and security, our CRM and messaging platform (HighLevel / LeadConnector) and the carriers and aggregators that deliver text messages, scheduling tools, form processors, analytics, and AI technology providers. They may use the information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety purposes, where required by law or legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and property.
  • Business transfer to a successor in a merger or sale of the business, subject to this Policy.

All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the text message services.

7. Data retention and security

We keep personal information for as long as needed for the purposes above, typically for the life of the business relationship plus a reasonable period, unless law requires longer. We keep records of text message consent and opt-outs as needed to honor your choices and show compliance. We use commercially reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

You may opt out of texts by replying STOP and of emails by using the unsubscribe link at any time. Depending on where you live, including under the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act and similar state laws, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to appeal a decision on your request. Email hello@whosystem.com or call (757) 705-5468. We will verify your identity and respond as required by law.

9. Cookies and analytics

You can control cookies through your browser settings. If we use analytics or advertising tools, they may collect usage data as described in their own policies, and you can opt out using tools such as the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or your platform's ad preferences.

10. Children

The Site is intended for business audiences and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Third-party sites

The Site links to third-party websites and services (scheduling, review platforms, social media) that have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the current version, and material changes will be posted on this page.
